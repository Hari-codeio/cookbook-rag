# Red Pepper Shakshuka

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red peppers | 450 g | deseeded, sliced |
| Brown onion | 200 g | sliced |
| Tinned plum tomatoes | 400 g | |
| Olive oil | 40 g | |
| Garlic | 15 g | sliced thin |
| Sweet paprika | 6 g | |
| Ground cumin | 4 g | |
| Fine sea salt | 8 g | |
| Eggs | 6 whole | room temperature |
| Flat leaf parsley | 15 g | to finish |

## Method

Soften the peppers and onion in the olive oil over a low flame for twenty minutes. They
should slump rather than colour. Add the garlic, paprika and cumin and stir for one minute.

Add the tomatoes and salt, break them down with the back of a spoon, and simmer uncovered
until the sauce holds a channel when you drag a spoon through it — about fifteen minutes.

Make six wells with the back of a ladle and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook over low
heat for six to eight minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks still move. Scatter
the parsley and serve straight from the pan.

## Allergens

Contains egg. Prepared in a kitchen that handles wheat, dairy and sesame.
