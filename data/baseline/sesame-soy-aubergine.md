# Sesame and Soy Roasted Aubergine

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aubergine | 800 g | halved lengthways, scored |
| Light soy sauce | 60 g | |
| Mirin | 45 g | |
| Toasted sesame oil | 20 g | |
| Caster sugar | 15 g | |
| Rice vinegar | 15 g | |
| Ginger, grated | 15 g | |
| Toasted sesame seeds | 12 g | to finish |
| Spring onion | 40 g | sliced on the bias |

## Method

Score the cut faces of the aubergine in a close diamond pattern, going most of the way
through the flesh but not the skin. Salt them lightly and leave cut side down on paper for
fifteen minutes to draw off water.

Whisk the soy, mirin, sugar, vinegar and ginger into a glaze. Brush a third of it into the
scored flesh and roast cut side up at 200 C for 25 minutes.

Brush again and roast for a further ten minutes, until the flesh is completely collapsed and
the surface lacquered. Finish with the sesame oil off the heat — heating it destroys the
aroma — then the sesame seeds and spring onion.

## Allergens

Contains soy (soy sauce), sesame and wheat (most soy sauce contains wheat; use tamari for a
gluten free version).
